## DOI policy

This repository does **not** use a separate software DOI. The DOI field is reserved for the **published paper only**.

When the journal DOI becomes available, update:

1. `CITATION.cff` → add the official DOI under `preferred-citation`;
2. `CITATION.bib` → replace the provisional manuscript entry with the final article metadata;
3. this file → add the final journal citation;
4. `README.md` → optionally add a direct paper citation block.

Do not invent volume, issue, pages/article number, publication date, or DOI before they are assigned.
